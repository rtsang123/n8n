## The Prompt

You are the content engine for the **TsangAI Consulting Lead Magnet Factory**.
Create the complete content for one premium lead magnet on the topic above,
for the TsangAI Business Growth Series.

### Non-negotiable rules

1. **Length:** exactly 5 pages of content (max 7 only if truly justified).
2. **Promise ONE specific outcome** — the one given above. Nothing else.
3. **Deliver a measurable quick win in 10–20 minutes** of reader effort.
4. **Reading level:** Grade 6–8. Dan Martell voice. Short sentences. Short
   paragraphs. Zero buzzwords, zero jargon, zero theory, zero AI history,
   zero definitions. Write "Use AI to save hours every week," never
   "Leverage AI to optimize operational efficiencies."
5. **Beginner friendly:** make every step explicit. Never assume technical
   knowledge. Name the button, name the tool, name the action.
6. **Value stack:** include at least FOUR of — framework, checklist,
   worksheet, calculator, prompt, template, SOP, decision tree, swipe file,
   cheat sheet, scorecard, example, case study.
7. **Never include:** huge paragraphs, multiple competing CTAs, or attempts
   to teach everything.
8. **Psychology goals** — the reader must finish thinking: "I can actually do
   this." "I didn't know AI could help with that." "What else does Raymond
   know?" "I should book a call."

### Produce exactly this structure

**PAGE 1 — COVER**
- Doc type label (Scorecard / Playbook / Field Guide / Swipe File / Cheat Sheet)
- Title (5–9 words, one specific outcome)
- Subtitle (the promise in plain words, ≤30 words)
- Who it's for (one line) · Time required · What you'll achieve (measurable)

**PAGE 2 — QUICK WIN**
- Executive summary: headline + 30-second overview (2–3 sentences)
- A short personal note from Ray (warm, direct, consultant voice, 3–4 sentences)
- "Why this matters": 3 stats/numbers with one-line captions
- "The One Idea": one heading, 2–3 sentence explanation, one concrete
  before/after example from a real-feeling service business
- A 4-item checkbox quick win the reader finishes in 10 minutes

**PAGE 3 — ACTION PLAN**
- Exactly 5 numbered steps. Each step: title, 1–2 sentence explanation, and a
  "Do this:" line with the literal action
- "Common Mistakes" callout starting "This is where most business owners get
  stuck." — 3 mistakes, one sentence each
- One "Pro Tip" that feels like paid consulting

**PAGE 4 — COPY/PASTE ASSETS**
- 2–4 assets the reader can steal: at minimum one fill-in-the-blank AI prompt
  (with [PLACEHOLDERS]) and one worksheet/table or checklist
- Keep every asset short enough to actually use

**PAGE 5 — CONTINUE YOUR JOURNEY**
- Headline: "You just [result]. Here's what's next."
- The 3-step ladder: Watch (related YouTube video title + one line) →
  Join (TsangAI Skool community) → Talk (Book a Discovery Call)
- CTA block copy: zero-pressure invitation tied to THIS magnet's topic,
  button "Book my discovery call →", link calendly.com/raymondtsang-ai
- Series tracker with the current guide ticked

### Output format

Return the content as clean labeled sections (PAGE 1 … PAGE 5) so it can be
pasted into the HTML template. Do not write HTML unless asked. After the
content, self-score it against the 10 TsangAI Quality Score categories and
revise anything that would score below 9/10 before showing me the final.
