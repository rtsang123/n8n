# The TsangAI Quality Score™

Every lead magnet is scored out of 100 before it ships.
**Anything under 90/100 gets revised. No exceptions.**

Score each category 0–10. Be harsh — readers will be.

| # | Category | What a 10 looks like | Score |
|---|----------|----------------------|-------|
| 1 | Solves ONE specific problem | The title names one outcome; nothing off-topic made it in | /10 |
| 2 | Gives immediate win | A reader gets a measurable result today, in ≤15 minutes | /10 |
| 3 | Step-by-step instructions | Exactly 5 steps, each with a literal "Do this:" action, no skipped steps | /10 |
| 4 | Beginner friendly | Zero assumed technical knowledge; tools and buttons are named | /10 |
| 5 | Easy to skim | Grade 6–8 reading level; short sentences; a skimmer still gets the value | /10 |
| 6 | Professional design | Feels like Apple/Stripe: whitespace, hierarchy, brand navy + blue, no walls of text | /10 |
| 7 | Practical worksheets | At least one table/worksheet the reader physically fills in | /10 |
| 8 | Copy/paste templates | At least one prompt or template with [PLACEHOLDERS], short enough to actually use | /10 |
| 9 | Creates curiosity for audit | Reader finishes thinking "what else does Raymond know?" — CTA ties to the topic | /10 |
| 10 | Strong CTA | One ladder (YouTube → Skool → Call), zero competing CTAs, zero pressure | /10 |
| | **Total** | **Ship at 90+** | **/100** |

## The 5 gates (instant fail if any is a NO)

- [ ] Can someone finish it in under 15 minutes?
- [ ] Will they get a measurable result today?
- [ ] Is it so useful they'd send it to another business owner?
- [ ] Does it naturally create curiosity for the AI Audit?
- [ ] Does it build trust instead of just showing knowledge?

## Fast revision guide

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Score low on #1 | Cut sections until only the promised outcome remains |
| Score low on #2 | Shrink the quick win: what can they finish before lunch? |
| Score low on #5 | Read it aloud; split every sentence you breathe through |
| Score low on #6 | Delete text before adding design; whitespace is the design |
| Score low on #9 | Make the CTA continue the exact topic, not a generic pitch |
