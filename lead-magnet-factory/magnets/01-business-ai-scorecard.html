The One Idea

Diagnose first. Automate second.

AI works best when you point it at one clear bottleneck. When you skip the diagnosis, you buy tools that collect dust. When you score first, every dollar and hour you invest lands where it matters.

Example: A landscaping company owner was sure he needed AI marketing. His scorecard said otherwise — quoting was his lowest score. One AI quoting assistant later, he cut quote time from 45 minutes to 8, and closed 30% more jobs simply by replying first.

The Action Plan

Five steps from score to first win

1

Take the scorecard

Rate all 10 statements on page 4. Don't overthink — your first instinct is usually right.

Do this: Print page 4 or write scores on your phone. Total them up.

2

Find your lowest section

Each pair of statements covers one area: Time, Marketing, Sales, Operations, and AI Basics. Your lowest pair is your bottleneck.

Do this: Circle the section with the lowest combined score.

3

Name the single worst task

Inside that section, pick the one task that eats the most hours or loses the most money each week.

Do this: Write one sentence: "Every week I lose ___ hours to ___."

4

Run the AI analysis prompt

Paste the prompt on page 4 into ChatGPT or Claude along with your scores. It will suggest your first automation, step by step.

Do this: Copy, paste, fill in the blanks, hit enter.

5

Block 30 minutes this week

Knowledge without a calendar slot changes nothing. Book time to try the first suggestion while it's fresh.

Do this: Open your calendar now and book "AI Fix #1" this week.

Common Mistakes

This is where most business owners get stuck.

They score every area a 4 because honesty stings. They try to fix all five areas at once. Or they buy a tool before naming the problem. Score low, pick one, fix one.

Pro Tip

Re-take this scorecard on the first Monday of every month. A rising score is the cleanest proof your AI investments are actually working — and a flat score tells you to change course before you waste another quarter.

What your total means

  • 0–15 — Manual Mode. Huge upside. Start with one fix.
  • 16–30 — Getting There. Pick your lowest area and go deep.
  • 31–42 — Systemized. Time to automate, not just organize.
  • 43–50 — AI-Powered. You're ahead. Now scale it.
