The Action Plan

Five steps to never losing a warm lead

1

Find your real response time

Send a test enquiry through your own website form right now. Time how long until a human replies. That number is your baseline — and probably your biggest leak.

Do this: Submit your own form. Write down the hours (be honest).

2

Install the instant reply (Touch 1)

Every new lead gets a friendly, useful reply within minutes — confirming you got their message and telling them exactly what happens next.

Do this: Set the Touch 1 template as an auto-reply on your form, or a one-click snippet on your phone.

3

Load the full 5-touch sequence

Touches 2–5 are on page 4: value, proof, a nudge, and a polite breakup. Personalize each once with AI, then save them as templates.

Do this: Run the Personalizer Prompt on page 4, paste results into your email templates or CRM.

4

Put the sequence on rails

Follow-up dies when it depends on memory. Use CRM reminders at minimum — or automate the sends with your CRM, Zapier, or n8n so touches go out on schedule unless the lead replies.

Do this: Set the schedule: Touch 1 instant, 2 on day 2, 3 on day 5, 4 on day 9, 5 on day 14.

5

Track one number weekly

Leads in vs. jobs booked. That's your conversion rate. Watch it move as the sequence does its work — and stop guessing whether marketing "is working."

Do this: Add a two-column tally to your Friday routine: leads this week, booked this week.

Common Mistakes

This is where most business owners get stuck.

They quit after one follow-up because "I don't want to be pushy" — while the buyer simply forgot. They send "just checking in" messages that add zero value. And they follow up from memory, which means only when it's quiet.

Pro Tip

The breakup message (Touch 5) gets the most replies of the whole sequence. "Should I close your file?" forces a decision — and buyers who were stalling suddenly find their wallet. Never skip it.

Copy / Paste Assets

The 5-touch sequence — steal every word

Touch 1 · Instant reply (0–5 min)

Hi [NAME] — thanks for reaching out about [SERVICE]! I got your message and will get back to you with details by [TIME] today. If it's urgent, call or text [PHONE]. — [YOUR NAME]

Touch 2 · The value follow-up (day 2)

Hi [NAME], following up on your [SERVICE] enquiry. Quick thing worth knowing: [ONE USEFUL TIP OR ANSWER TO A COMMON QUESTION]. Happy to walk you through your options — does [DAY/TIME] work for a quick call?

Touch 3 · The proof (day 5)

Hi [NAME] — we just wrapped a [SIMILAR JOB] for a client in [AREA]: [ONE-LINE RESULT]. Here's what they said: "[SHORT REVIEW QUOTE]." Want me to put together the same plan for you?

Touch 4 · The easy nudge (day 9)

Hi [NAME] — I know things get busy. If you're still thinking about [SERVICE], just reply with a number: 1 = ready to book, 2 = have a question, 3 = need more time. Takes two seconds.

Touch 5 · The breakup (day 14)

Hi [NAME] — I don't want to clutter your inbox, so this is my last note. Should I close your file for [SERVICE]? If timing's just off, no problem — tell me when to check back and I'll set a reminder. Either way, thanks for considering us.
