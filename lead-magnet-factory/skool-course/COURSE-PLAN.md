# TsangAI Business Growth — Skool Course Plan

**Course name:** The AI Business Quick-Start
**Promise:** In 4 weeks: know your score, win back hours, book more jobs, and get your first automation live.
**Format:** 9 short videos (2–5 min each) + one PDF per lesson + one community action per lesson.

## Why these 7 PDFs (of 16)

Ranking rule: **money made > money saved > infrastructure**, and every lesson
must be finishable in one sitting. The course order builds from habit to
revenue to systems, and ends with members shipping a real automation — the
strongest possible proof that the community delivers.

**Camera vs Loom logic:** camera for the three moments that carry trust and
emotion (welcome, first lesson, capstone + wrap-up). Loom screen-shares for
PDF walkthroughs — seeing the pages and prompts on screen actually teaches
better than a talking head.

## The engagement loop (why every lesson has a post action)

Skool rewards activity. Every lesson ends with "post one thing" —
a score, a template result, a sentence. This:
1. Keeps the community feed alive without you creating extra content.
2. Creates social proof for lurkers ("people are getting wins here").
3. Tells you what members need next: if half the scorecards say "my lowest
   area is Sales," you know exactly what next month's playbook and live
   workshop should cover.

## Script format (used in COURSE-SCRIPTS.md)

Every script follows the same beat, tuned for 2–5 minutes at a natural
~140 words/minute:

1. **Hook** (10–15s) — the pain, in their words
2. **Why this matters** (30s) — one stat or story
3. **Walkthrough** (60–120s) — what's in the PDF, page by page
4. **⏸ ACTION PAUSE(s)** — literal instruction to pause the video and do the thing
5. **Community CTA** (15s) — what to post
6. **Handoff** (10s) — what the next lesson does

Delivery notes: talk like the PDFs read — short sentences, no buzzwords,
Grade 6–8. Pauses are announced explicitly ("Pause the video here. I mean
it — this only works if you do it now.").
