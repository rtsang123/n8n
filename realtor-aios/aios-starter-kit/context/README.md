# Your Context Files

This folder is your AI's memory of your business. The files here are created
by the modules you install (starting with `context-os`) and are plain text —
you can open and read any of them.

You never need to edit these by hand: tell Claude what changed ("I moved
brokerages", "I'm focusing on Willoughby now") and it updates the right file.

Files that will appear here as you install modules:

| File | What it holds | Created by |
|------|---------------|------------|
| `business-profile.md` | Who you are, brokerage, team, credentials | context-os |
| `market.md` | Your market, farm area, price bands, conditions | context-os |
| `clients.md` | Your niche and the client types you serve | context-os |
| `listing-process.md` | How you take a listing from consult to close | context-os |
| `buyer-process.md` | How you guide buyers from first call to keys | context-os |
| `content-history.md` | Where you've posted, what worked, your channels | context-os |
| `positioning.md` | How you want to be seen, and what you'd never say | context-os |
| `voice-profile.md` | Your captured writing voice | brain-clone |
| `samples/` | Pieces of your past content used to learn your voice | brain-clone |
