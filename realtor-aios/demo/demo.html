the setup

The whole install is four typed lines

No terminal. No configuration. Your consultant sets up Claude Code with you, you open your workspace, and each module installs itself when you type its line — then interviews you in plain conversation.

01/install-module context-os

Learns your business — market, clients, process. A 30–45 min interview.

02/install-module brain-clone

Learns your voice from your past content plus a brain-dump chat.

03/install-module marketing-engine

Topic research and your weekly Facebook + LinkedIn content.

04/install-module lead-engine

Lead capture at intake and follow-up that never slips.

About two hours end to end, with natural coffee breaks — and it's guided, enforced in the right order, and impossible to break by typing the wrong thing. If you can send a text message, you can run this. Everything it learns is saved as plain files on your computer, readable any time.
