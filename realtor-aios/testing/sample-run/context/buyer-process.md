# Buyer Process

*From first call to keys, with an emphasis on first-timers feeling in control.*

## From first call to keys
1. First call — understand what they actually want, not just the checklist
2. Get them talking to a broker early if not already pre-approved
3. Tour efficiently — don't drag people through 15 homes that don't fit
4. Walk through every offer term in plain language before they sign anything

## What I do differently for buyers
Explain subject-free offers honestly, including the real risk, instead of
pushing them into one to win a bidding war.

## Protecting buyers
Always recommend an inspection unless the buyer explicitly understands and
accepts the tradeoff of waiving it. Never let a deposit go anywhere without
walking through what it means.
