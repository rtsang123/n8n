# Clients

*Mostly first-time buyers and young families, with a growing downsizing/relocation share.*

## Who I serve, ranked
1. First-time buyers — the bulk of the business
2. Move-up families
3. Downsizers and relocations — smaller share, growing

## Ideal client
Someone who's done a little homework already, is realistic about their
budget, and wants a straight answer instead of a sales pitch.

## Where business comes from
- Referrals and sphere — largest share
- Social (Facebook/Instagram) — growing
- Portal inquiries — steady

## Questions clients ask over and over
- "Is now a bad time to buy?"
- "What's a subject-free offer and is it risky?"
- "How much do I actually need for a deposit?"
