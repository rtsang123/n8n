# Listing Process

*Straightforward, pricing-honest, no fluff in the marketing.*

## From first conversation to sold
1. Walk the home, talk pricing honestly before anything else
2. Professional photos + a short walkthrough video, always
3. List, promote across Facebook/Instagram, open house first weekend if it fits
4. Review offers with the seller line by line, no pressure either direction

## What I do differently
Tell sellers the real number, not the number they want to hear. Costs me
some listings up front, saves everyone a bad two months later.

## Pricing philosophy
Price to the data, not to the seller's Zillow-adjacent gut feeling. Reprice
fast if the market says so instead of waiting it out.
