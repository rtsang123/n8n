# Market

*Langley and the Fraser Valley, BC — farm area centred on Willoughby.*

## Farm area
- Primary: Willoughby, Willowbrook
- Secondary: Murrayville, Walnut Grove (adjacent, works there regularly too)

## What I actually sell
- Mostly townhomes, some detached, occasional condo
- Typical price band: $650K–$950K
- Buyer mix skews first-time buyers and young families

## What's true right now
- "Wait for a crash" sentiment is loud online but not reflected in what's
  actually happening — well-priced townhomes in Willoughby are still moving
  in under two weeks
- Buyers have gotten pickier about price, not scarcer — priced right sells,
  priced on hope sits
- Willoughby has newer-build inventory that pulls buyers away from older
  Willowbrook stock unless the older stock is priced to compensate

## What outsiders get wrong
- Assuming the whole Fraser Valley moves together — Willoughby and
  Willowbrook behave differently even though they're next to each other
