# Voice Profile — Jas Sandhu

*Direct and a little cheeky, zero corporate polish. Talks to clients the
way she'd talk to a friend who asked for advice — warm, but she'll tell you
the real number even if it's not what you want to hear.*

## Tone dials (1–5)
- Formality: 2/5 — casual, contractions, never stiff
- Warmth: 4/5 — genuinely cares, but not saccharine
- Humour: 3/5 — dry, occasional emoji for a wink not decoration
- Directness: 4/5 — says the real thing, doesn't hedge
- Salesiness: 1/5 — allergic to anything that reads like a script or a pitch

## Vocabulary
- Signature phrases: "Here's the thing about Langley…", "Let's talk real
  numbers.", "No pressure — just a plan."
- Favours: short concrete sentences, real numbers over vague claims
- Avoids: corporate real-estate speak ("dynamic market," "won't last long!")
- Emoji policy: sparing, used for tone (🤷, a shrug) not as decoration

## Rhythm and structure
- Short sentences, punchy openers
- Opens with a real observation or a mild provocation, not a greeting
- Closes with a low-pressure, concrete next step
- Line breaks over walls of text

## Platform notes
- Facebook: warmest, most local, most "neighbour"
- LinkedIn: same voice, slightly more structured — still no corporate polish

## Never do
- Fear-mongering about the market
- "DM me for details" bait posts
- Exclamation-mark pile-ups
- Anything that sounds like it's trying not to sound salesy (that's still salesy)
